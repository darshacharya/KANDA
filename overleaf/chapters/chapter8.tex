\chapter{Experimental Results and Conclusions}
\label{ch:results}

This chapter reports how \textbf{Knowledge-driven Autonomous Navigation and Decision-making Agent (KANDA)} behaved in bench demonstrations after the tests defined in Chapter~\ref{ch:testing}. Because the deployment is a physical cyber-physical system rather than a batch benchmark, results combine \emph{qualitative} operator observations (motion, mode switches, recovery) with \emph{configuration-backed} timing figures and UART protocol checks. Section~\ref{sec:eval_setup} describes the setup; Section~\ref{sec:results_outcomes} summarises scenario outcomes; Section~\ref{sec:timing_metrics} records timing and stack parameters; Section~\ref{sec:discussion} discusses limitations; Section~\ref{sec:ch8summary} states conclusions and future work.

%-------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:eval_setup}

\textbf{Hardware.} ESP32 DevKit with three HC-SR04 sensors (with dividers on echo lines), TB6612FNG dual driver, SSD1306 OLED, differential-drive chassis, and Raspberry Pi connected via UART (115200 baud). Power and pinout follow project documentation.

\textbf{Software.} Firmware sketch \texttt{phase2\_obstacle\_avoidance.ino}; intelligence layer from \texttt{ai\_layer/} with default \texttt{gemini-2.5-flash} unless overridden. Tests ST-01--ST-03 from Chapter~\ref{ch:testing} provide the procedural baseline.

\textbf{Ethics and safety.} Trials were run on a clear bench with the platform elevated or wheels clear of pinch points; an operator remained ready to disconnect power. No public-road or crowded-environment trials were conducted.

\textbf{Narrative expectations.} In AUTO\_MODE, the robot should reduce forward motion when the front range drops below the configured obstacle threshold and should favour turns consistent with left/right clearance. In AI\_MODE, OLED and logs should show JSON-derived actions within the whitelist; after prolonged UART silence, behaviour should match standalone avoidance again. Under faults, logs should show stop or skip-with-warning without sustained unsafe drive~\cite{huang2023grounded}.

The intelligence loop is therefore deliberately \emph{slower} than the inner firmware sense--act rate: the LLM plans at a few hundred millihertz while local reflexes operate faster~\cite{vemprala2024chatgpt,brooks1986robust}. This separation is intentional: it avoids assuming sub-second cloud latency for safety-critical reactions while still allowing contextual decisions when the link is healthy.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{Sensing.} Ultrasonic sensors suffer multipath, absorption on soft materials, and blind zones; the prompt’s distance heuristics are approximations, not collision guarantees.

\textbf{Cloud dependence.} AI\_MODE requires HTTPS reachability and valid credentials; performance and availability follow the provider’s SLA, not the robot’s real-time schedule.

\textbf{Validation scope.} The validator enforces discrete verbs and speed bounds but does not prove temporal logic (e.g.\ ``never reverse into a closing gap'') without richer models or sensors.

\textbf{Evaluation rigour.} Without instrumented ground truth (motion capture, lidar map), results remain demonstration-grade; expanding logged telemetry and repeatable courses would strengthen quantitative claims.

\textbf{Broader context.} Service robotics growth motivates accessible embodied stacks~\cite{ifr2024report}; KANDA’s contribution is educational and architectural---commodity hardware with an explicit safety boundary---rather than a new foundation model~\cite{brohan2023rt2}.

\section{Conclusions and Future Work}
\label{sec:ch8summary}

\textbf{Conclusions.} KANDA implements a dual-processor embodied stack: an ESP32 closes the loop on ranging and motors with AUTO\_MODE fallback, while a Raspberry Pi runs Gemini~2.5 Flash behind a hardware-aware prompt and JSON validator. Newline-framed UART messages keep debugging simple; the whitelist and speed clamp implement a practical enforcement layer between stochastic language output and PWM~\cite{ahn2022saycan,huang2023grounded}. Demonstrations confirmed that the architecture matches the phased objectives set out in Chapter~\ref{ch:intro}: grounded commands, validation before motion, and graceful degradation when the brain or link is absent.

\textbf{Future work (Phase~4).} Camera and microphone inputs, on-device buffering of traces, richer maps or SLAM, and optional local small models for offline planning are natural extensions, consistent with multimodal composition ideas~\cite{zeng2023socratic,wu2023tidybot}. Formal verification of the validator against a labelled command grammar, hardware-in-the-loop regression, and structured user studies would further strengthen evidence.

This closes the technical narrative of the project; the bibliography lists the literature that informed design and evaluation choices.
